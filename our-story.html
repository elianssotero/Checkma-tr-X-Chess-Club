01 / THE BEGINNING

WHERE IT ALL STARTED

A Simple Idea.
A Purpose Worth Pursuing.

Every meaningful journey begins with an idea—and sometimes, that idea starts with just one person.

CheckmaîtréX Chess Club began as a simple, unexpected idea: to create a space that could encourage more people to discover chess, learn its principles, and improve their skills. Without an established club or a large team at the beginning, the project started with a vision and the willingness to bring it to life.

What began as a personal initiative took shape through the creation of its website and its developing identity. The purpose was clear: to help make chess more approachable and inspire others to appreciate the game beyond the board.

CheckmaîtréX is built on the belief that a great beginning does not require everything to be in place. It starts with a reason to begin, a willingness to learn, and a vision worth pursuing.

02 / OUR IDENTITY

A NAME WITH CHARACTER

The Meaning Behind
the Name.

The name CheckmaîtréX takes inspiration from one of the most recognizable concepts in chess: checkmate.

Its spelling gives the familiar word a distinctive identity. Inspired by the French word maître, associated with mastery, the name incorporates the letter X for a modern, bold, and stylish finish. The accented “é” adds another visual detail, helping the name stand apart.

Together, these elements reflect two sides of the CheckmaîtréX identity: the pursuit of skill and improvement in chess, and a refined visual style that makes the club recognizable.

Its black-and-gold aesthetic completes this identity, bringing together elegance, confidence, and the strategic spirit of the game.

More than a name created to stand out, CheckmaîtréX represents a chess project with a clear purpose: to encourage people to explore the game, develop their abilities, and discover the value of every move.

03 / THE JOURNEY AHEAD

A VISION FOR THE FUTURE

A Bigger Board.
A Growing Community.

The vision of CheckmaîtréX Chess Club reaches beyond building a recognizable name. It is about helping more people in the Philippines discover chess, learn to play, and find reasons to keep improving.

While many people are more familiar with widely followed sports, chess deserves opportunities to reach new audiences too. CheckmaîtréX hopes to encourage greater interest in the game and contribute, in its own way, to a growing and welcoming chess community.

The journey ahead is an opportunity to make chess more approachable, share the value of learning, and inspire people to experience the game for themselves. Every new person who becomes interested in chess is a meaningful step toward that vision.

The club is still developing, and its future will be shaped over time. What remains at the heart of its ambition is a simple belief: chess should be something more people have the chance to discover, enjoy, and grow through.

One board. One move. One growing community at a time.
