Membership Overview

Begin Your Next Move

Learn about the values and process behind joining CheckmaîtréX Chess Club.

01

Who Can Apply?

Anyone interested in chess and the club's community may express their interest through the membership application. Final eligibility is subject to the club's membership requirements and review.

02

What Can You Expect?

Membership is intended to support a shared interest in chess, learning, strategic thinking, discipline, and sportsmanship. Specific activities and benefits depend on the club's programs.

03

Application Review

Every application must be reviewed according to the club's process. Submitting the form alone does not confirm membership or guarantee approval.

How It Works

Membership Application Process

Follow these steps to submit your application and understand what happens afterward.

  1. Complete the Application Form Open the official Google Form and provide the requested information accurately.
  2. Submit Your Application Review your answers before submitting the form. Keep any submission confirmation for your reference.
  3. Wait for the Club's Review Your application must be reviewed before your membership can be officially approved.
  4. Receive Your Official Membership Details If approved, follow the club's instructions for receiving your official membership confirmation and Membership ID, when issued by the club.

Important Membership Notice

Submitting a membership application does not automatically make an applicant an official member. Membership is subject to review and approval by CheckmaîtréX Chess Club. A Membership ID should be considered official only after it has been issued or confirmed by the club.
