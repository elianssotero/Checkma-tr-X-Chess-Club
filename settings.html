Help & FAQ

How do I change the website theme?

Choose a theme from Appearance. Your selection is saved on this device and previews immediately on this page.

Why do some pages still show the old colors?

Some pages may contain hardcoded colors. Their CSS must be updated separately for all 11 themes to look consistent.

Does the language option translate every page?

No. It saves your preference. A translation system must be implemented separately.

Are notifications sent automatically?

No. These options save your preferences only. Real notifications need a notification service.

What does Reset All Settings do?

It removes the known website preference keys and reloads the page. It does not intentionally delete saved AI conversations.
