Help & FAQ

How do I change my website theme?

Choose a theme under Appearance. The selected theme is applied immediately and saved on this device.

Why do some parts of the website keep their old colors?

Some pages contain hardcoded colors. They may need additional CSS updates before every part follows all 11 themes.

Do notification switches send real notifications?

No. They currently save your preferences. A notification service or backend is needed to send real notifications.

Does choosing Filipino translate every page?

No. The language preference is saved, but full translation must be implemented separately.

Where are my settings stored?

These preferences are stored in this browser's local storage on this device. They are not automatically synced to another device.

What does Reset All do?

It removes the settings keys used by this page and restores the defaults when the page reloads. It does not clear your entire browser cache.
