Meet the Founder

El.ians Sotero

Founder & Club President
Arena International Master (AIM)

El.ians Sotero, an Arena International Master (AIM), is the founder and president of CheckmaîtréX Chess Club. Driven by a passion for chess and a commitment to promoting the game, he established the initiative to encourage a wider appreciation of chess and contribute to the development of a welcoming chess community in the Philippines.

As the club's founder and president, he guides its identity, digital presence, and long-term direction. His vision is to make chess more approachable, encourage continuous learning, and inspire people to appreciate the strategic thinking, patience, and discipline that the game can cultivate.

As CheckmaîtréX Chess Club continues to develop, his leadership remains focused on building a strong foundation, expanding awareness of chess, and pursuing a future grounded in learning, improvement, and community development.

Like chess itself, leadership requires patience, thoughtful decisions, adaptability, and a willingness to keep learning. These principles guide the effort to develop CheckmaîtréX Chess Club steadily and responsibly.
